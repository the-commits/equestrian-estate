Bespoke Brass Hardware

Hand-finished latches, hinges and name plates.

Every latch is cast in solid brass, brushed by hand and left unlacquered so it darkens slowly with use. The double-action stall latch opens with one hand and cannot be lifted by a curious muzzle.

  • Solid cast brass
  • Horse-proof double action
  • Engraved name plates

Detail 02 / 03

Radiant Heated Flooring

Warm, dry footing through the winter.

Low-temperature water circuits run beneath the brick pavers and stall mats, keeping the floor dry and gently warm on frosty mornings without drying the air.

  • Underfloor water circuits
  • Brick pavers on lime screed
  • Rubber stall mats

Detail 03 / 03

Natural Airflow Design

Fresh air without draughts.

Ridge vents and high clerestory openings draw warm air up and out while fresh air enters low through the grilles, so every stall is ventilated without a draught at horse height.

  • Continuous ridge vent
  • Opening clerestory
  • Draught-free at horse height

Heritage & Architecture

Built by hand, for a century of mornings

Oak trusses raised in 1898 still carry the roof. We kept every beam that could be kept, re-pointed the limestone with lime mortar and let the clerestory windows do what they were made for: bring the first light of the day straight down the aisle.

  • 02Lime-mortared limestone
