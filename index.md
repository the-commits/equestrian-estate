Heritage & Architecture

## Built by hand, for a century of mornings

Oak trusses raised in 1898 still carry the roof. We kept every beam that could be kept, re-pointed the limestone with lime mortar and let the clerestory windows do what they were made for: bring the first light of the day straight down the aisle.

- Hand-hewn oak trusses
- Lime-mortared limestone
